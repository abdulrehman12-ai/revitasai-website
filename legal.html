Required notices

Terms, privacy, cancellation, and non-PHI boundaries

This page identifies what is implemented in software and what still requires operator or legal approval. It is not a substitute for counsel.

Non-PHI pilot

The Founding Pilot is a non-PHI operational workspace. It is not HIPAA compliant, not approved for PHI, not a licensed CPT or coding product, and not a live paid AI-provider service.

Subscription and cancellation

The software plan is USD $60 per month per team, including up to five members, with a seven-day trial. Checkout collects a payment method and charges $0 at signup. Stripe attempts the first $60 charge when the trial ends unless the customer canceled through the Stripe billing portal before then.

Refund, chargeback, tax nexus, Stripe Tax, and card-network trial-reminder copy are not decided in this repository. Do not treat this page as an approved refund policy.

Privacy and support

A customer privacy policy, DPA, and subprocessors list require legal review before live billing. Support contact: support@revitasai.com.

Account identity

Paying at Stripe does not create a Studio login. Access requires a verified invite-only identity and an explicit non-PHI policy acceptance. Public self-signup remains disabled.
